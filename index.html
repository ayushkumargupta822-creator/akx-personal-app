ⓘ About AKX

A personal space to plan, learn and stay organised.
AKX Personal OS
Welcome to AKX Study Library

AKX is a personal productivity and study companion designed to bring your everyday academic work and personal planning together in one place.

Use Study to track learning, Books to keep your study files organised, Tasks and Routine to manage your day, and Notes, Goals and Stats to review your progress. The AKX AI section helps you prepare study prompts and explore important topics.

Your workspace, your plans, your progress — organised in one place.

Created by

Ayush Kumar Gupta

Founder & creator of AKX Personal OS

A quick note

AKX is a personal project. Your entries are stored on this device unless you use an export or another configured backup method. The AI Study Prompt Studio prepares prompts; it does not currently generate AI answers inside the app.
